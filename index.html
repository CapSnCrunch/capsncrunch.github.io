Why does my site look like this?

I made this site by hand while in college, before AI-coding assistants came out shortly after, and before I was even considering becoming a software engineer, as a way to document and share the things I found interesting to work on. I kept it relatively simple, partly because I had not learned about JS frameworks at that point and partly because I liked the sort of 90s web-minimalism of it.

If you're looking to work with me as a SWE, I promise not all of my websites look like this. Take a look at my projects for a bit of a better view of the type of stuff I make now.
