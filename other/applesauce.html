Shortly after our wedding on Pi Day, Linda told me she wanted to try starting an art business. She had already been working on a few designs and had some ideas of what she wanted to work on next. Over the course of a few months, she made tons of coolest looking stickers and even an entire Insect Zine for all ages! With my software engineering background, I was able to build her a fully functioning ecommerce site.

Since the brand focuses on the concepts of 'wonder' and 'playfulness', I spent a lot of time making all the nice little micro-interactions, UX, and general design of the site.

Take a look and order while we still have these items in stock! I'm sure you'll find something you like. Join The Applesauce newsletter if you want to keep an eye out for new designs.
